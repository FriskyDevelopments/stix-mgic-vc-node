# Offer: free room-admin + sticker overlay to existing customers

Pitch to current VC Node customers: the room administration commands and the sticker overlay are shipping free, no upsell, as a thank-you for being early.

---

## The offer

**To every active $19 hosted subscriber (and every self-host template user):**

- Room admin panel (mute, kick, pin, end call) — free, included.
- Sticker overlay pipeline — free, included.
- Apple Music artwork via the Folio-sourced id — free, included.

No new SKU. No "Pro" gate on these three. They land in the base product.

---

## Why free

1. **Retention over extraction.** These customers are the ones who stuck around through the ghost-join era. Charging them for the moderation tools they asked for is how you lose them to the next host.
2. **The real upsell is elsewhere.** Extra operator slots, dedicated LCH VPS, higher media capacity, BYOK AI — those stay paid. Admin + overlay are table stakes for a DJ product; making them free makes the paid tiers feel earned instead of nickel-and-dimed.
3. **Word of mouth.** "They gave me room admin for free" is a sentence that travels. "They charged me $4/mo for mute" is not.

---

## How to deliver it

- Email / DM the active list: "room admin + sticker overlay are live, no action needed, already in your node."
- Dashboard: the admin panel appears automatically when a Telegram VC is live — no toggle, no upgrade prompt.
- ToS / changelog: note the addition under "included in every plan."
- Do **not** announce it as a limited-time promo. Permanent inclusion reads as generosity; a countdown reads as a funnel.
